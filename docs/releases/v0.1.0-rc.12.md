# Waycloak v0.1.0-rc.12

RC.12 is the exact support-matrix certification release candidate. It preserves
RC.11's provider-ingress firewall correction, the frozen
`networking.waycloak.io/v1beta1` API, the generic Gluetun integration boundary,
the port-forward lease contract, and the last-resort workload-adapter protocol.

The signed release manifest now carries the deliberately narrow certified
operator row: K3s `v1.36.1+k3s1`, Flannel, containerd `2.2.3-k3s1`, Linux 5.10
or newer, `amd64`, and Gluetun configured for Proton/OpenVPN. Its feature list
includes TCP, UDP, contained DNS, single-active provider port forwarding, and
the workload-adapter handoff. Named evidence suites bind the claim to the
turnkey install, datastore-recovery, and live qBittorrent/Proton journeys.

Kind, k3d, `arm64`, and multi-platform OCI availability remain useful test or
artifact evidence; they do not silently expand operator support. New support
rows require separately declared conformance evidence.

RC.12 must be consumed by exact signed identity and deployed through the
reviewed GitOps path. Its final graduation evidence is a fresh minimum 72-hour
unchanged-artifact soak on the existing local cluster with qBittorrent as the
sole application canary. Bitmagnet remains scaled to zero and no new cluster
node is required. This prerelease does not claim that soak or stable graduation
until the epoch completes.
