in each approved workload namespace. Do not grant it through a
ClusterRoleBinding.

approved gateway namespace binds the waycloak-gateway-secret-reader ClusterRole
to ServiceAccount {{ include "waycloak.fullname" . }} in namespace
{{ .Release.Namespace }}. Never bind that role cluster-wide.

Install one Waycloak Helm release per cluster; the CRDs, admission policy, and
persona ClusterRoles are cluster-scoped product identities.
